Artist profile 01 / 03

A sound born
from the noise.

Alice Glass is a Canadian artist whose distinctive vocals meet the darker, high-energy side of electronic music.

Known for her work with Crystal Castles, Glass brings raw electronic textures and emotional intensity into her solo work. This vlog is a short listening journal, tracing the path from her time in the group to the music she makes on her own.
